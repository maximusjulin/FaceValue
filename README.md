# 💵 Face Value

**Find out if your cash is worth more than its face value.**

Face Value is a mobile-friendly tool that helps users identify potentially valuable U.S. currency. Simply take a picture of a bill, and Face Value analyzes information to estimate whether the bill may have collectible value.

## 🚀 What It Does

Most people assume a $1 bill is worth exactly $1. However, certain older, rare, or unusual bills can be worth significantly more to collectors.

Face Value makes it easier for anyone to discover that hidden value.

**Scan → Identify → Learn → Estimate**

## ✨ Features

- 📸 **Bill Scanner** — Upload or take a picture of a U.S. bill
- 🔎 **Bill Identification** — Identifies important characteristics of the bill
- 🔢 **Serial Number Analysis** — Looks for potentially interesting serial-number patterns
- 📅 **Series Identification** — Uses the series/year to help determine rarity
- 💰 **Value Estimate** — Provides an estimated collector value
- 📚 **Learn About Your Bill** — Explains why a bill may be interesting or valuable
- 📱 **Mobile-Friendly** — Designed to be easy to use from your phone

## 🎯 The Problem

People carry potentially collectible currency every day without realizing it.

Determining whether a bill is valuable can require researching multiple factors, including:

- Age
- Series
- Serial number
- Printing characteristics
- Condition
- Rarity
- Errors or unusual features
- Collector demand

This information can be difficult for someone unfamiliar with currency collecting to understand.

## 💡 Our Solution

Face Value combines these factors into one simple experience.

Instead of manually searching through currency databases and collector guides, users can scan their bill and receive an easy-to-understand analysis.

> **Your money might be worth more than you think.**
